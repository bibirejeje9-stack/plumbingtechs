Our Story

Three Generations of Plumbing Excellence

Plumbing Techs of Michigan — legally Pipecon, Inc. — is a family-owned plumbing, construction, remodeling, and service company that has been in business for over 40 years. Scott Baxter and his son Shawn oversee a thriving operation with a database of more than 6,000 customers.

Scott’s father, James, was a plumbing contractor too — serving the needs of their customers runs in their blood. Originally located in Hazel Park, we moved to Oak Park in 2015 and opened a second location in Wixom to better serve the west side.

As a full-service company, we handle everything from small repair jobs like drain cleaning and scoping, all the way up to large-scale construction and renovation projects. We’re proud of our 4.9 Google rating — it means we’re doing something right.
