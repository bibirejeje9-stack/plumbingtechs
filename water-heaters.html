Water Heater Repair, Replacement & Installation

Has your water heater left you out in the cold? Plumbing Techs is the most trusted hot water heater company in Southeast Michigan, with over 40 years of experience.

If your water heater isn’t producing enough hot water, is leaking, making unusual noises, or has stopped working altogether, professional service can restore your home’s comfort — and prevent more costly plumbing problems.

At Plumbing Techs we service both traditional tank and tankless water heaters, helping homeowners restore reliable hot water with honest recommendations and quality workmanship. Our goal is to help you make an informed decision — not to recommend a replacement before it’s necessary.

Signs Your Water Heater Needs Attention

  • Age: most water heaters last 8–12 years. Beyond that, plan on replacing it soon.
  • Rusty water: rust from the hot tap often means the tank is corroding internally.
  • Leaking: moisture around the base can mean a cracked tank about to fail.
  • Inconsistent temperature: fluctuating or lukewarm water signals end of life.
  • Noises: rumbling, popping, or banging usually means sediment buildup.

Repair or Replace?

Not every problem means a new system. Replacing a heating element, thermostat, or pressure relief valve can restore reliable hot water. But if your unit is nearing the end of its lifespan, needs frequent repairs, or has a leaking tank, replacement is often the more dependable and cost-effective long-term solution.

Our promise: after a thorough inspection we’ll explain the cause of the problem, discuss your repair and replacement options, and help you choose what fits your home, budget, and long-term plans.

Benefits of Regular Maintenance

An annual flush removes sediment that causes rumbling and shortens equipment life. Routine checkups catch small issues before they become failures, lower your energy bills, and help protect your manufacturer’s warranty.

Choosing the Right Water Heater

The best option depends on your household’s hot water demand, available space, energy-efficiency goals, and whether your home is equipped for gas or electric service. We’ll help you compare traditional tank, high-efficiency, and tankless systems — and size the unit correctly so you’re not paying for more capacity than you need.
