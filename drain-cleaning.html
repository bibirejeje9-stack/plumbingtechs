Drain Cleaning

Professional Drain & Sewer Cleaning

The safest and most effective way to clear a stubborn drain is to hire a professional. Plumbing Techs has 40+ years of experience and a 2-year labor warranty.

Book an Appointment (248) 548-7488

A slow drain never fixes itself. Left alone, a small clog becomes a backed-up sink, a flooded floor, and a much bigger repair bill. Our team clears the blockage and finds the cause so it doesn’t keep coming back.

Signs You Need Professional Drain Cleaning

  • Water drains slowly in sinks, tubs, or showers
  • Gurgling sounds from drains or the toilet
  • Unpleasant odors coming from the drain
  • Water backing up in multiple fixtures at once
  • Repeat clogs that keep returning

Why Professional Cleaning Beats Store-Bought Cleaners

Chemical drain cleaners can damage older pipes and rarely clear a blockage all the way. A store-bought snake handles simple clogs near the surface but usually can’t reach a blockage deep in the main line — and forcing it can scratch or crack pipes. A professional-grade auger or hydro jetter reaches farther and clears tougher buildup safely.

Drain Snaking vs. Hydro Jetting

MethodBest ForWhat It Does
Drain snaking (cable auger)Single, localized clogsBreaks a hole through the blockage
Hydro jettingGrease buildup & root intrusionScours the full pipe wall clean

Hydro jetting typically costs more — often $300 to $600 depending on pipe length and access — but it restores full pipe diameter rather than just poking a hole through the clog. For repeat clogs in older Oak Park homes, we inspect the line with a camera first and then recommend the right approach.

What to Expect

Diagnose

We locate the blockage and, when needed, run a camera to see exactly what we’re dealing with.

Clear

We use the right tool — auger or hydro jetter — to clear the line completely and safely.

Verify & advise

We confirm full flow and give you practical advice to keep the drain clear.

Preventing Future Clogs

  • Use drain guards in sinks and tubs to catch hair and debris
  • Never pour grease or cooking oil down the drain
  • Run hot water after each use to keep lines flowing
  • Schedule periodic professional cleanings for older homes
